## Conciseness Checklist


Could I use simpler words?
Could I use fewer words? Are there redundant words that don’t add meaning?
Am I using zombie nouns? 
Am I stacking a bunch of nouns together?
Could this sentence be in active voice?
Could the subject and verb be closer?
Can I state this positively and more directly?
Could this sentence be split in two?


Could I remove “there is”, “there are”?
Could I make this text into a table or list?
Could I break up this wall of text?
Are there enough headings and signposts to make navigating this doc easy?
Does this doc contain more than one user goal or task? Could it be split up?
Am I being too concise? Am I sacrificing clarity for conciseness?
